# 0.1.0 implementation contract

## Visual foundation

One dark theme, platform default font, fixed semantic tones: Neutral `#333333`, Accent `#FFEA00`, Primary `#008BFF`, Success `#00CC0D`, Warning `#FFC300`, Danger `#C01C00`, Info `#CCCCCC`. There is no palette or global motion-disable API.

Shared control presets are Compact (40 dp / 14 sp), Default (52 dp / 20 sp), and Comfortable (62 dp / 24 sp). ZenlessTheme supplies the group size; each control can override it. Buttons, icon buttons, text fields, selects, switches, checkbox/radio rows, slider rows, ordinary tabs/navigation, information rows and collapse headers use this minimum height. Field horizontal paddings are 17 / 22 / 26 dp; ordinary button padding is 1.7 times that value. Icon slots are 22 / 28 / 34 dp. Checkbox/radio indicators are 22 / 28 / 34 dp and remain smaller than their interaction row. Badge, notice and heading typography retain their separate roles. These values are logical dimensions; text scaling may increase measured height. Business icons belong in content slots. Checkbox checks, mixed marks, close, back and dropdown arrows are intrinsic geometry.

All general button tones and variants, including standalone icon buttons, share a `#262626` shell with a `#3D3D3D` upper bevel. Enabled text is white, disabled text is `#565657`, and held text is black. Ordinary faces are inset by 8.35% of the shorter edge from the rim bounds; leading-icon faces use measured connected cutouts. Disabling does not change the shell. Dark faces have black / `#090909` checks at a 4.8 dp period. Filled leading-icon buttons keep a dark face: the tone colors a badge at the left edge (Danger `#FF2B00`, Warning `#FFB000`), with black caller artwork. Buttons with and without a leading icon share the same platform-default typography: normal weight, no forced italics, 1 sp letter spacing, and 14 / 20 / 24 sp text for Compact / Default / Comfortable. Alert actions explicitly use Default typography with their independent 56 dp minimum height. Disabled badges retain 35.3% of their color. Leading-button labels are centered between the leading circular slot's far edge and the trailing semicircle's center (mirrored in RTL). Symmetric padding of 12 dp at Default, proportional to the preset height, within this area keeps text outside the icon circle, including at natural button widths; labels without an icon are centered in the whole button. The interior half of the leading ring is flat gray with soft edges, formed with the outer frame as one continuous silhouette. Only the exterior shell has a bevel. Its static plate is cached using 4x sampling; press, pulse and release feedback remain animated above it.

Control text inherits its size preset (20 sp by default). Explicit Body / Title / Subtitle / Caption / Number styles are 24 / 42 / 28 / 20 / 44 sp; text inside a button inherits its state ink. Other enabled plates have a 1 dp outer inset, 3 dp semantic edge, black separator and face inset of 5 dp. Inputs and other disabled plates use a 4 dp face inset. Their ordinary texture repeats every 6 dp; colors interpolate by channel. Back and close plates are 90 by 60 dp cubic contours, with contour-normal expansion during presses. The back plate has a separately measured inner contour, a `#C50600` filled arrow and rim, an upper-left bevel, and soft black / `#090909` checker cells repeating every 4.806 dp. The close plate has independently measured outer, separator and face contours, a `#C50600` rim, red `#C50600` / `#CB1100` checker cells repeating every 4.8 dp, and upper-left bevels on both rim and face. Its black cross has 6.601 dp strokes centered at (40, 29.389) in the nominal plate, with 0.35 dp edge softness. Slider knobs are concentric metal discs, 24 dp at Default, scaled by the indicator-size ratio for other presets. Their tracks remain thin and vertically centered; numeric inputs share the control row height.

Comfortable switches use a separate 142 by 62 dp recessed capsule. Compact and Default uniformly scale this geometry to 40 and 52 dp high, including the ON/OFF artwork and thumb travel. The 112.25 by 33.5 dp face is `#272727` when off and `#36A100` when on, with black condensed ON/OFF vector lettering. The 42 dp black thumb surround contains a 33.5 dp metal disc and travels 86.5 dp. Optional labels precede the track on a connected dark capsule, use platform text at the preset font size (24 sp for Comfortable) with inherited font weight, and wrap when constrained. The whole labeled control toggles; disabled controls retain their shell and use muted ink and green.

Folder tabs occupy an 84 dp rail, with 18 dp top corners and square bottom corners. Their unbranded faces are redrawn vector artwork. A single-line Alert has a centered 186 dp band and 272 by 56 dp actions centered across its lower edge; content grows for multiple lines and actions adapt to narrow screens. Alert actions reuse `ZenlessButton` with `leadingIcon`, including its shell, state ink and interaction feedback. Drawer width is 46% of the viewport, capped at 680 dp, or full width below 600 dp. It has a 96 dp header and a draggable 8 dp scroll thumb.

## State and motion

Non-folder tabs have circular caps on the first and last outer edges, with slanted interior edges. A single tab has two circular caps. `ZenlessTabs(expand = false)` keeps the selected indicator's 1.5-second color cycle without outline expansion; the default is `true`. Folder tabs ignore this option.

Non-folder selection uses one shared indicator, moving over 280 ms with `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Its caps interpolate between circles and slanted rounded corners as it leaves or approaches either end. Interrupted transitions retarget from the current position, and color/scale breathing keeps its phase. Text is black only where the moving indicator covers it. Pointer presses do not add another highlight or release flash; the selected indicator stays visible and follows selection changes. `expand = false` removes the scale pulse, not selection movement or morphing. Item-count, folder-style or layout-direction changes reset the indicator to the current selection.

- Controlled values, selection and visibility are supplied by callers. Radio groups are caller-owned; wrap grouped radios in `selectableGroup()`.
- Checkbox toggling maps Off or Indeterminate to On, and On to Off. Switch toggles on click/tap; dragging is not a switch interaction.
- Switch motion follows the supplied recording's frame timestamps: opening moves the thumb over 133 ms with cubic easing `(0.2, 0, 0.2, 1)`, then brings in green over 83 ms after a 50 ms delay. Closing fades green over 33 ms before the same thumb travel. The departing legend disappears immediately; the arriving legend fades in over 67 ms after a 67 ms delay. Switches have no yellow plate pulse or release flash. Reversing during travel starts from the current animated values.
- Disabled/loading buttons suppress activation. Loading reserves the existing content footprint. Delayed back/close and Alert actions guard repeat activation.
- The back arrow changes from `#C50600` to black with the pressed highlight, remains black throughout a hold, follows the release flash, and restores its resting red after release or cancellation.
- Held buttons use black text, including explicit Caption text. Standalone and inline icons use black ink; icons inside a leading black disc instead follow the plate's yellow-green pulse in phase. `ZenlessButton.leadingIcon` and Alert action badges use that leading-disc rule. Caller-drawn icons read the read-only `zenlessContentColor` inside their slot. Tabs, navigation rows, cards, select triggers/options and text fields do not use button press highlights or release flashes; their selection, focus and expanded-state feedback remains. Checkbox and RadioButton use their 160 ms value transition without a button flash. Resting, disabled and loading colors remain distinct from a held state.
- Held action buttons and selected horizontal tabs use a 1.5-second yellow-to-green cycle. Outline expansion is 15% of the shorter edge, with a 150 ms attack and 640 ms repeating pulse. Only action buttons (ordinary, leading-icon, standalone icon, back/close, Alert actions and collapse headers) use a 150 ms double release flash, including a down/up within one rendered frame. Selected ordinary button borders, options and vertical navigation use a 2-second color cycle without expansion. Folder tabs shift 7 dp and interpolate their face/text colors over 160 ms.
- Select opens over 200 ms with 32 dp translation, closes over 100 ms, scrolls the selected row into view, and flips/clamps against the viewport. Trigger height follows the preset; option minimum height is the preset plus 2 dp (42 / 54 / 64 dp). Popup height and selected-row scrolling follow that option size. The trigger uses a separate rounded filled triangle, nominally 16 by 13 dp at Comfortable and proportional to control height in the other presets. It sits at the trailing semicircle center (mirrored in RTL), with white enabled ink and #787879 disabled ink. Collapse and scroll-hint arrows retain their own shapes.
- Alert background blur is live, up to 4.5 dp, with a black mask at 0.58 opacity. Opening is staged over 250 ms; closing lasts 220 ms. The mask blocks background interaction through exit. Drawer slides in over 280 ms with cubic ease-out and out over 220 ms with cubic ease-in. Its 0.40-opacity scrim fades over 180 / 220 ms, and its content scrolls independently of the header/footer.
- Tooltip accepts supplementary text. Mouse hover delay is 600 ms; touch long press uses the platform threshold. Outside tap, scrolling the anchor away, or another tooltip dismisses it. No interactive or essential content belongs inside.
- Text input delegates IME, selection and clipboard behavior to Compose. No custom keyboard navigation/shortcuts or gamepad mapping is added.

## Gallery

Chinese and English follow the system language initially and can be toggled. Wide windows use a category sidebar; narrow windows show categories and detail separately. Examples use public library APIs. Parameter changes update the matching code snippets. Example state is ephemeral and resets when leaving a category. Code copying operates on the visible example.

## Acceptance

Build coverage and visual acceptance are separate. Automated checks cover state transitions, disabled/loading actions, modal exit callbacks, popup placement, timing curves, generated strings and responsive gallery screenshots. Native platform launch checks verify startup. Final motion fidelity and touch behavior still need human review on real devices; desktop screenshots and simulator startup are not substitutes.

The unit-density geometry fixture captures 1920 by 1080 pixels without labels. The test suite generates all Gallery category screenshots plus Alert, drawer and geometry captures under the ignored `verification/` directory. Default fonts, device scale and rasterizer antialiasing differ by platform.

The enabled/disabled leading-button fixture renders 244 by 58 dp controls at density 2. Native reference crops are 488 by 116 pixels, starting at (132, 1999) and (673, 1999) in the supplied 3840 by 2160 screenshot. After excluding the label rectangle and central icon disc, RGB mean absolute error is 0.62 for disabled and 0.80 for enabled (0–255 per channel); the previous disabled shell measured 7.06 under the same mask. The mask excludes x=136..414, y=26..89 and the circle centered at (59, 58.5) with radius 35. Regression checks cover the rim brightness, reference contour scanlines, flat interior arc and its soft edge, unchanged disabled shell/texture, badge colors and non-italic default text. These are static calibration measurements, not a claim of identical rasterizer antialiasing. Reference captures are not bundled or committed.

The leading ring's top and bottom joins are also compared separately in 35 by 21 pixel regions at (70, 5) and (70, 92). Mean RGB error fell from 0.65 / 0.71 to 0.46 / 0.45; the largest channel difference is 6/255 at a soft edge. All 385 solid join pixels (x=70..104, y=5..9 and 107..112) now match `#262626` exactly in both enabled and disabled fixtures, with no dark overlap line. Tests also cover square leading buttons and leading-side placement under RTL.

The back button uses the upper-left navigation control in the supplied 3840 by 2160 screenshot as its separate reference. Its fixture renders at density 2 into a 188 by 128 pixel crop, placing the 90 by 60 dp control at (4, 4). The matching reference crop starts at (128, 40). RGB mean absolute error decreased from 18.15 to 1.75 over that crop; on the union of pixels with a channel above 10 it is 5.08. Red-mask intersection over union is 97.92% (red > 100, green < 50). These static measurements include the arrow and texture. Regression assertions cover reference scanlines within one pixel, the red fill, bevel and checker samples. Edge antialiasing remains different from the captured raster; the reference image is not bundled with the library.

The close button uses the upper-right control in `绝区零 2026_9_27 13_07_42.png` (3840 by 2160). Its density-2 fixture is 188 by 128 pixels, with the button at (4, 4); the reference crop starts at (3524, 38). Full-crop RGB mean absolute error decreased from 16.46 to 2.46 (0–255 per channel), or 23.30 to 3.45 over the union of pixels with a channel above 10. Red-mask intersection over union increased from 81.49% to 98.53% (red > 100, green < 50). Tests cover sampled contour and cross scanlines within one pixel, both red checker colors, both bevels, the separator, layout size, expansion, held ink, delayed activation, cancellation and disabled input. Antialiasing still differs, especially at the nearly horizontal contour ends; this is a measured static calibration, not bit-identical raster output or cross-platform visual acceptance. The reference capture and comparison images stay in ignored `verification/`, outside the distributed library.

The switch fixture uses the lower-right controls in the supplied 2026-10-10 05:54:44 (off) and 05:54:48 (on) screenshots. At density 2 its 284 by 124 pixel track matches crops starting at (2803, 1995). Full-crop RGB mean absolute error is 4.18 off / 4.07 on (0–255 per channel); inside the capsule, excluding the surrounding game background, it is 3.23 / 3.50. That mask uses pixel coordinates with `dx = max(62 - x, x - 222, 0)` and `dx² + (y - 62)² < 60²`. The track measurements include the metal disc and ON/OFF artwork, but exclude the separate platform-font label. Residual metal shading and raster antialiasing differ, so these measurements do not establish literal pixel identity. The PNGs provide the static color target; the 06:40:24 recording provides motion timing. Tests cover both endpoints, contour scanlines within one pixel, measured travel, asymmetric staging, press/cancel/disabled behavior, rapid reversal, caller updates, and narrow layouts with increased font scale. Reference images and generated comparisons remain under ignored `verification/toggle/`.

The shared-size regression fixture checks all three presets at density 1 and 2, runtime size changes, explicit overrides, enlarged text, menu selection/scrolling, and the independent back/folder geometry. `verification/control-sizes.png` shows the three groups together. Gallery size selection is included in copied Kotlin and checked against the live controls.
