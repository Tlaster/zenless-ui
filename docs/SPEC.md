# 0.1.0 implementation contract

## Visual foundation

One dark theme, platform default font, fixed semantic tones: Neutral `#333333`, Accent `#FFEA00`, Primary `#008BFF`, Success `#00CC0D`, Warning `#FFC300`, Danger `#C01C00`, Info `#CCCCCC`. There is no palette or global motion-disable API.

Shared control presets are Compact (40 dp / 14 sp), Default (52 dp / 20 sp), and Comfortable (62 dp / 24 sp). ZenlessTheme supplies the group size; each control can override it. Buttons, icon buttons, text fields, selects, switches, checkbox/radio rows, slider rows, both tab styles/navigation, information rows, collapse headers and Alert actions use this minimum height. Field horizontal paddings are 17 / 22 / 26 dp; ordinary button padding is 1.7 times that value. Icon slots are 22 / 28 / 34 dp. Checkbox/radio indicators are 22 / 28 / 34 dp and remain smaller than their interaction row. Back/close plates use the preset height and a 3:2 aspect ratio: 60 by 40, 78 by 52, and 93 by 62 dp. Spinners, badges, notices, progress bars, metrics, tooltips, card insets, navigation caps and Alert/drawer spacing scale by height / 52 while retaining their separate proportions. Cards and drawers also pass their size override into content slots. Explicit heading/body/caption/number text roles remain independent of control size. These values are logical dimensions; text scaling may increase measured height. Business icons belong in content slots. Checkbox checks, mixed marks, close, back and dropdown arrows are intrinsic geometry.

All general button tones and variants, including standalone icon buttons, share a `#262626` shell with a `#3D3D3D` upper bevel. Enabled text is white, disabled text is `#565657`, and held text is black. Ordinary faces are inset by 8.35% of the shorter edge from the rim bounds; leading-icon faces use measured connected cutouts. Disabling does not change the shell. Dark faces have black / `#090909` checks at a 4.8 dp period. Filled leading-icon buttons keep a dark face: the tone colors a badge at the left edge (Danger `#FF2B00`, Warning `#FFB000`), with black caller artwork. Buttons with and without a leading icon share the same platform-default typography: normal weight, no forced italics, 1 sp letter spacing, and 14 / 20 / 24 sp text for Compact / Default / Comfortable. Alert actions inherit the same size and typography as ordinary buttons. Disabled badges retain 35.3% of their color. Leading-button labels are centered between the leading circular slot's far edge and the trailing semicircle's center (mirrored in RTL). Symmetric padding of 12 dp at Default, proportional to the preset height, within this area keeps text outside the icon circle, including at natural button widths; labels without an icon are centered in the whole button. The interior half of the leading ring is flat gray with native-antialiased edges, formed with the outer frame as one continuous silhouette. Only the exterior shell has a bevel. The bevel direction is normalized to the button dimensions so wider labels retain the upper highlight; circular icon buttons retain their separate lighting. A black surround extends 2 dp outside the layout, so clipping parents need to reserve that space. The leading button static plate is cached at native resolution; press, pulse and release feedback remain animated above it.

Control text inherits its size preset (20 sp by default). Explicit Body / Title / Subtitle / Caption / Number styles are 24 / 42 / 28 / 20 / 44 sp; text inside a button inherits its state ink. Other enabled plates have a 1 dp outer inset, 3 dp semantic edge, black separator and face inset of 5 dp. Inputs and other disabled plates use a 4 dp face inset. Their ordinary texture repeats every 6 dp; colors interpolate by channel. Back and close plates scale their calibrated 90 by 60 coordinate system to the preset height, including checker cells, bevels, intrinsic glyphs and edge softness, with contour-normal expansion during presses. The back plate has a separately measured inner contour, a `#C50600` filled arrow and rim, an upper-left bevel, and soft black / `#090909` checker cells repeating every 4.806 dp. The close plate has independently measured outer, separator and face contours, a `#C50600` rim, red `#C50600` / `#CB1100` checker cells repeating every 4.8 dp, and upper-left bevels on both rim and face. Its black cross has 6.601 dp strokes centered at (40, 29.389) in the nominal plate, with 0.35 dp edge softness. Slider knobs are concentric metal discs, 24 dp at Default, scaled by the indicator-size ratio for other presets. Their tracks remain thin and vertically centered; numeric inputs share the control row height.

Comfortable switches use a separate 142 by 62 dp recessed capsule. Compact and Default uniformly scale this geometry to 40 and 52 dp high, including the ON/OFF artwork and thumb travel. The 112.25 by 33.5 dp face is `#272727` when off and `#36A100` when on, with black condensed ON/OFF vector lettering. The 42 dp black thumb surround contains a 33.5 dp metal disc and travels 86.5 dp. Optional labels precede the track on a connected dark capsule, use platform text at the preset font size (24 sp for Comfortable) with inherited font weight, and wrap when constrained. The whole labeled control toggles; disabled controls retain their shell and use muted ink and green.

Folder tabs occupy the same 40 / 52 / 62 dp minimum rail height as ordinary tabs and use the preset font size. Their calibrated 84 dp geometry (18 dp top corners, square bottom corners, bottom rail and face artwork) scales by height / 84. Their unbranded faces are redrawn vector artwork. At Default, a single-line Alert has a centered 186 dp band and 272 by 52 dp actions centered across its lower edge; band, spacing and action widths scale with the preset, while actions share the standard control height; content grows for multiple lines. Action widths can grow to fit their labels; actions wrap when they cannot fit side by side on a narrow screen. Alert actions reuse `ZenlessButton` with `leadingIcon`, including its shell, state ink and interaction feedback. Drawer width is 46% of the viewport, capped at 680 dp, or full width below 600 dp. At Default it has a 96 dp header and a draggable 8 dp scroll thumb, both scaled with the preset. The viewport-based drawer width and responsive breakpoints do not change with control size.

## State and motion

Non-folder tabs have circular caps on the first and last outer edges, with slanted interior edges. A single tab has two circular caps. `ZenlessTabs(expand = false)` keeps the selected indicator's 1.5-second color cycle without outline expansion; the default is `true`. Folder tabs ignore this option.

Non-folder selection uses one shared indicator, moving over 280 ms with `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Its caps interpolate between circles and slanted rounded corners as it leaves or approaches either end. Interrupted transitions retarget from the current position, and color/scale breathing keeps its phase. Text is black only where the moving indicator covers it. Pointer presses do not add another highlight or release flash; the selected indicator stays visible and follows selection changes. `expand = false` removes the scale pulse, not selection movement or morphing. Item-count, folder-style or layout-direction changes reset the indicator to the current selection.

- Controlled values, selection and visibility are supplied by callers. Radio groups are caller-owned; wrap grouped radios in `selectableGroup()`.
- Checkbox toggling maps Off or Indeterminate to On, and On to Off. Switch toggles on click/tap; dragging is not a switch interaction.
- Switch motion follows the supplied recording's frame timestamps: opening moves the thumb over 133 ms with cubic easing `(0.2, 0, 0.2, 1)`, then brings in green over 83 ms after a 50 ms delay. Closing fades green over 33 ms before the same thumb travel. The departing legend disappears immediately; the arriving legend fades in over 67 ms after a 67 ms delay. Switches have no yellow plate pulse or release flash. Reversing during travel starts from the current animated values.
- Disabled/loading buttons suppress activation. Loading reserves the existing content footprint. Delayed back/close and Alert actions guard repeat activation.
- The back arrow changes from `#C50600` to black with the pressed highlight, remains black throughout a hold, follows the release flash, and restores its resting red after release or cancellation.
- Held buttons use black text, including explicit Caption text. Standalone and inline icons use black ink; icons inside a leading black disc instead follow the plate's yellow-green pulse in phase. `ZenlessButton.leadingIcon` and Alert action badges use that leading-disc rule. Caller-drawn icons read the read-only `zenlessContentColor` inside their slot. Tabs, navigation rows, generic cards, select triggers/options and text fields do not use button press highlights or release flashes; their selection, focus and expanded-state feedback remains. Checkbox and RadioButton use their 160 ms value transition without a button flash. Resting, disabled and loading colors remain distinct from a held state.
- Held action buttons and selected horizontal tabs use a 1.5-second yellow-to-green cycle. Outline expansion is 15% of the shorter edge, with a 150 ms attack and 640 ms repeating pulse. Only action buttons (ordinary, leading-icon, standalone icon, back/close, Alert actions and collapse headers) use a 150 ms double release flash, including a down/up within one rendered frame. Selected ordinary button borders, options and vertical navigation use a 2-second color cycle without expansion. Folder tabs shift 7 * height / 84 dp and interpolate their face/text colors over 160 ms.
- Select opens over 200 ms with 32 dp translation, closes over 100 ms, scrolls the selected row into view, and flips/clamps against the viewport. Trigger height follows the preset; option minimum height is the preset plus 2 dp (42 / 54 / 64 dp). Popup height and selected-row scrolling follow that option size. The trigger uses a separate rounded filled triangle, nominally 16 by 13 dp at Comfortable and proportional to control height in the other presets. It sits at the trailing semicircle center (mirrored in RTL), with white enabled ink and #787879 disabled ink. Collapse and scroll-hint arrows retain their own shapes.
- Alert background blur is live, up to 4.5 dp, with a black mask at 0.58 opacity. Opening is staged over 250 ms; closing lasts 220 ms. The mask blocks background interaction through exit. Drawer slides in over 280 ms with cubic ease-out and out over 220 ms with cubic ease-in. Its 0.40-opacity scrim fades over 180 / 220 ms, and its content scrolls independently of the header/footer.
- Tooltip accepts supplementary text. Mouse hover delay is 600 ms; touch long press uses the platform threshold. Outside tap, scrolling the anchor away, or another tooltip dismisses it. No interactive or essential content belongs inside.
- Text input delegates IME, selection and clipboard behavior to Compose. No custom keyboard navigation/shortcuts or gamepad mapping is added.

## Feed cards

`ZenlessFeedCard` has five required composable slots: cover and avatar have bounded Box scopes; author, title and summary inherit the card's default text styles without line limits. Category label and status are optional Row scopes with built-in capsule backgrounds. Null hides a capsule; an empty composable retains its background. The parent supplies width and a finite positive cover width/height ratio reserves the image region before loading. Image cropping, avatar artwork/background, business icons, loading/error presentation and text overflow belong to the caller. Content slots must be non-interactive; the whole card merges their semantics and has one action. Reading status is arbitrary caller content and never automatically dims the card.

The external selected state, native press and input focus are combined into one continuous two-second rim color cycle using the existing signal palette. State handoffs do not restart it. There is no scale pulse, hover highlight or release flash. Native focus and keyboard activation are supported for this component; no custom focus-navigation or gamepad mapping is added. Disabling suppresses activation and breathing and applies 50% opacity. Inner geometry and default typography scale by the existing size preset; parent width and the cover ratio do not.

## Pill containers

`ZenlessPillContainer` owns only the opaque shaded shell, its external highlight and a clipped `RowScope` content slot. Content contains no independent controls. Width follows content or caller constraints; 40 / 52 / 62 dp presets are minimum shell heights. At Default, padding is 18 dp horizontally and 6 dp vertically; padding, edge widths and inherited text size follow the preset. Taller content grows the capsule; explicit fixed constraints clip the contents. No avatar, text layout, progress bar, business assets or palette overrides are part of the component.

Optional `onClick` supplies one whole-container action; null leaves display-only content. External selection, native press and keyboard focus share `Motion.signalColor`'s two-second cycle. Handoffs preserve the running phase. Release/cancellation returns to selected/focused state, never toggles selection automatically, and has no flash. Hover does not highlight. Disabling suppresses activation and breathing without fading the shell or caller content. The highlight extends 3 dp outside the Default shell (proportional to preset); it does not affect measurement. A clipping parent must reserve this space. Content clipping is inside the highlight drawing layer.

## Gallery

Chinese and English follow the system language initially and can be toggled. Wide windows use a category sidebar; narrow windows show categories and detail separately. Examples use public library APIs. Parameter changes update the matching code snippets. Example state is ephemeral and resets when leaving a category. Code copying operates on the visible example.

## Acceptance

Build coverage and visual acceptance are separate. Automated checks cover state transitions, disabled/loading actions, modal exit callbacks, popup placement, timing curves, generated strings and responsive gallery screenshots. Native platform launch checks verify startup. Final motion fidelity and touch behavior still need human review on real devices; desktop screenshots and simulator startup are not substitutes.

The unit-density geometry fixture captures 1920 by 1080 pixels without labels. The test suite generates all Gallery category screenshots plus Alert, drawer and geometry captures under the ignored `verification/` directory. Default fonts, device scale and rasterizer antialiasing differ by platform.

The enabled/disabled leading-button fixture renders 244 by 58 dp controls at density 2. Native reference crops are 488 by 116 pixels, starting at (132, 1999) and (673, 1999) in the supplied 3840 by 2160 screenshot. After excluding the label rectangle and central icon disc, RGB mean absolute error is 0.62 for disabled and 0.80 for enabled (0–255 per channel); the previous disabled shell measured 7.06 under the same mask. The mask excludes x=136..414, y=26..89 and the circle centered at (59, 58.5) with radius 35. Regression checks cover the rim brightness, reference contour scanlines, flat interior arc and its soft edge, unchanged disabled shell/texture, badge colors and non-italic default text. These are static calibration measurements, not a claim of identical rasterizer antialiasing. Reference captures are not bundled or committed.

The leading ring's top and bottom joins are also compared separately in 35 by 21 pixel regions at (70, 5) and (70, 92). ## Feed card calibration

The baseline is Windows Desktop, density 2, font scale 1 and Default size. The original `绝区零 2026_10_10 8_27_23.png` is 3840 by 2160. The fixture renders 320 dp-wide cards and compares unscaled 640 by 931 pixel crops at (186, 416) and (893, 1167), plus a 640 by 700 status-card crop at (186, 1398). Its fixed caller slot heights isolate the component geometry from font layout. Actual application text remains unrestricted.

The shell uses three rounded corners, a square bottom-end corner, a continuous avatar crest, a `#181818` information panel and a `#2A2A2A` divider. Default geometry includes a 36 dp outer corner, a 66 dp avatar, a 36.5 dp crest radius and a 32.5 dp minimum capsule height. Fractional face placement is calibrated to the captured edge coverage, not inferred by treating source pixels as dp. Label/status backgrounds are drawn independently from the generic badge.

The measured RGB mean absolute errors (0–255 per channel) are **1.939 normal / 2.225 selected / 0.748 status** across the fixed component masks. The selected yellow contour has **99.583% intersection-over-union**. Panel/divider error is 0.218 / 0.789 / 0.238 respectively. The blue capsule's region is less exact (8.304), as are some avatar-crest and translucent edge pixels. These values are not literal pixel identity: the reference uses different antialiasing, and the approved existing breathing palette intentionally differs slightly from the captured yellow.

The comparison script preserves raw differences and documents all masks. Caller text, cover interiors and avatar artwork are excluded from component scores. To avoid baking the original UI into the fixture, preparation removes the source capsule/crest pixels from the input artwork and interpolates their occluded backing; uncertainty at translucent edges is included in the error. No source UI pixels are pasted over rendered results. Reference artwork and generated images stay under ignored `verification/feed-card/`, outside the library and Gallery distribution.

Reproduce locally with Pillow and NumPy installed:

```powershell

.\gradlew.bat :zenless-ui:desktopTest --tests '*FeedCardTest*'
python tools/compare_feed_card.py
```

Outputs include `comparison.png`, per-case `*-overlay.png`, masked `*-difference-x4.png`, unmasked `*-raw-difference-x4.png`, `*-mask.png` and `metrics.json`. The local reference-capture test skips when reference artwork is absent; geometry, state, focus/keyboard, scroll cancellation, cover reservation, all-size/density, unrestricted text and invalid-ratio tests always run. Gallery regression covers selection, capsule toggles, disabled state and copyable parameters. The library tests lock measured reference contour scanlines within one pixel and verify color-only motion. Other platform builds and real-device visuals are separate acceptance work.

## Resource-management button border recalibration

The supplied `绝区零 2026_10_10 16_38_03.png` is compared at its original 3840 by 2160 resolution. The 650 by 145 crop starts at (2435, 1980). The Desktop fixture places a 299 by 58 dp button at (13, 9.5) dp with density 2. Caller text/icon artwork is omitted; the fixed outer-rim mask excludes it, the badge, inner ring and most of the face. Separate upper-straight and left-curve measurements overlap the main rim mask. No reference pixels are rendered by the component.

The previous upper highlight faded too early on long buttons, and its backing did not extend beyond the layout as the captured black surround does. Normalized lighting and a 2 dp black surround correct those differences while retaining the #262626 rim, native-size cache and existing interaction feedback. Outer-rim RGB MAE decreased from **2.2431 to 1.4752**; the upper straight region decreased from **3.4994 to 0.3580** (0-255 channel units). The left curve remains less exact: **4.4223 to 4.5297**, mainly because native antialiasing is sharper than the source raster. This is a static border calibration, not pixel identity or cross-platform visual acceptance. Black-surround coverage is tested on a contrasting flat backdrop rather than scored against the game's patterned background.
